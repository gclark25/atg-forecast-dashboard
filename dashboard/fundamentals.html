How to read this

  • Timing. Metis timestamps its hours by their start, and ERCOT's hour-ending rows are converted to the same start, so a Metis 4:00 PM point is compared with ERCOT's HE17. The alignment check below confirms this from the data.
  • Different vintages. The two sides are issued on different schedules (Metis hourly from :00, ERCOT's reports around :55, :00 and :30), so part of any gap is simply newer information on one side. The feed health card shows how old each side is.
  • Percentiles. In the hour-by-hour tooltips, "sits at Metis p23" means ERCOT's number falls at the 23rd percentile of Metis's own forecast distribution for that hour. That is the strength of the signal for the system-level rows, which carry all 21 quantile levels.
  • Not covered. Metis's net imports have no ERCOT forecast to compare with, so they appear only in the curve chart.
